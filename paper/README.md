# DEE paper

- `dee.tex`, `refs.bib`: the paper source. `dee.pdf`: the built PDF.
- `figures/make_figures.py`: regenerates both figures from numbers in `dee.cpp/runs/*/scorecard.json`
  and `dee.cpp/theory/PREDICTIONS_PHASE6.md`.
- `make_arxiv.sh`: builds the PDF and `arxiv.tar.gz` (the file to upload to arXiv).
- `ARXIV_CHECKLIST.md`: what to fix, fill in and do to submit (Nivin submits).
- `HN_POST.md`: Hacker News title and first comment draft.
