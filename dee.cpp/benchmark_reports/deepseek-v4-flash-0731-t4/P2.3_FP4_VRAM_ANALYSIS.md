# P2.3 — Packed FP4 VRAM Residency: Analysis

Status: **implementation merged** (`aefe035`, branch
`freebuff/deepseek-v4-flash-0731-t4`), **trace-replay simulation complete**
(`p2_3_trace_replay_simulator.py`). T4 full-run validation pending (v21/v22
blocked by repeated 1-GPU Kaggle allocations).

## What P2.3 adds

`DeviceCacheDType::Fp4E2m1` — the VRAM expert cache stores the checkpoint's
**packed e2m1fn bytes + e8m0 scales verbatim** (12.75 MiB per real expert)
instead of the expanded FP16 blob (48 MiB). Expansion happens only at compute
time, into one bounded per-engine FP16 scratch, using the same
`fp4_e2m1_to_f16_cuda` kernel as the old transfer-stream path — so the FP16
values (and therefore tokens) are unchanged while the cache holds **3.76× more
resident experts per GiB**.

Wiring: `stage_expert` streams packed bytes via `prefetch_fp4_regions_packed`;
compute decodes via `decode_fp4_cache_block_to_scratch` in
`moe_forward_experts`, `moe_forward_batch_device`, and `forward_layer_cuda`.
Combined/pointer-batched paths fail closed on Fp16. Harness knob:
`NATIVE_CACHE_DTYPE=fp4`.

## Trace-replay results (sealed DS10 v12 token_trace, 16 tokens × 43 layers)

Accesses: 6,364 (5,676 routed + 688 shared). LRU with shared experts pinned.

| Budget | FP16 routed hit | FP4 routed hit | FP4 total hit | FP4 evicts | Belady (FP4) |
|--------|----------------|----------------|---------------|------------|--------------|
| 1 GiB  | 0.1%           | 4.4%           | 19.8%         | 5,064      | 27.5%        |
| 2 GiB  | 0.1%           | 15.4%          | 19.8%         | 4,984      | 42.4%        |
| 4 GiB  | 5.3%           | **29.1%**      | 40.2%         | 3,527      | 54.9%        |
| 6 GiB  | 11.6%          | 38.8%          | 44.2%         | 3,114      | 59.7%        |
| 8 GiB  | 16.6%          | **47.0%**      | 48.3%         | 2,690      | 62.2%        |

- **At the same budget, packed FP4 roughly triples the routed hit rate**
  (2 GiB: 0.1%→15.4%; 4 GiB: 5.3%→29.1%; 8 GiB: 16.6%→47.0%).
- **Reuse-distance coverage caps at 62.2%**: the 16-token trace's structural
  reuse (37.5% consecutive-token overlap) is bounded; the remaining accesses
  are first-seen cold. Belady at 8 GiB (62.2%) is the hard ceiling — LRU at
  packed FP4 reaches 47.0%, i.e. ~76% of what oracle eviction could do.
- **Bytes/token at the primary 3.5 GiB budget**: H2D drops from
  **14.95 GiB → 3.97 GiB/token** with packed FP4 (routed hits 3.5%→27.0%).

## Storage roofline with packed FP4 (43×6=258 routed loads/token, ~12.75 MiB each)

| Storage | FP16 (16.6% hit @8G) | FP4 (47% hit @8G) |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------|
| 13 MB/s (dataset loop) | 0.004 tok/s | 0.007 tok/s |
| 126 MB/s (SSD) | 0.038 tok/s | 0.062 tok/s |
| 710 MB/s (DEE4 repack) | 0.113 tok/s | 0.165 tok/s |

GPU compute ceiling stays ~0.30 tok/s (43×6×26 ms / 2 GPUs), so packed
residency + DEE4 storage + router-ahead prefetch (P2.8) are all needed to
approach it.

## Conclusion

1. Packed FP4 residency is a **real, measured capacity lever**: 3.76×
   residents, ~3× routed hit rate at equal budget, 3.7× less H2D/token at the
   primary budget. It does NOT require changing any numerical value.
2. It is **not sufficient alone** — the trace's reuse ceiling (62%) plus the
   storage wall means the pipeline (P2.4 batching, P2.8 prefetch, P2.5 fused
   decode) is still required to reach M3+.
3. Correctness risk is low by construction: same decode kernel, same FP16
   values, fail-closed on unsupported paths. The FP4 expert regression test
   now runs the packed-cache dtype against the FP32 reference.
